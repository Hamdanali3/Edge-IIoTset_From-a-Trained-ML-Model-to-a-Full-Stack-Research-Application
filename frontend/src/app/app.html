Full-Stack ML Security Research

Stopping Dataset Leakage in IIoT Intrusion Detection

Models on Edge-IIoTset often score 99%+ by reading file-export artifacts ("0" vs "0.0") and lab IPs. Removing those leaks drops accuracy to 88.97%. Extracting 24 privacy-preserving structural features (PP-PSFE) recovers accuracy to 96.49% without keeping any IPs, URLs, or raw payloads.

96.49% Test Accuracy (15 classes)
3.54 ms Per 1,000 packet flows
Step 1 · Audit & Clean

Remove Dataset Cheating

  • Unifies "0.0" → "0" across 7 columns
  • Fixes 93 shifted TShark DNS rows
  • Drops 8 dead fields, 5,414 duplicates & 15 lab IDs
Step 2 · Extract PP-PSFE

Keep Structure, Drop Identity

  • 9 direction-invariant port roles
  • 6 TCP options & entropy (0–4 bits)
  • 9 HTTP/MQTT lengths & attack flags
Step 3 · Train & Serve

Zero-Skew Production Bundle

  • Train-only OrdinalEncoder + StandardScaler
  • XGBoost on 62 features (152,386 flows)
  • Served live via Flask REST API & Angular
